### 1.2 Filing in India (Indian Patent Office - IPO) — Direct Online Portal
If you prefer filing first in India:
1. **Portal**: [https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/goForLogin/doLogin](https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/epatentfiling/goForLogin/doLogin)
2. **Form**: Select **Form 1** (Application for Grant of Patent) + **Form 2** (Provisional Specification).
3. **Upload**: Attach the text from `/legal-ip/PATENT_SPECIFICATION_PROVISIONAL.md`.
4. **Fees**: **₹1,600 INR** for natural persons / individual inventors.
5. **Result**: Immediate Indian Patent Application Number with priority date secured under the Paris Convention.
